A Summary of A Great and Terrible Beauty by Libba Bray

After her mother’s mysterious death in India, sixteen-year-old Gemma Doyle is sent to Spence Academy, a strict boarding school in England. Haunted by visions linked to her mother’s death, Gemma learns she has magical abilities tied to a secret society called the Order: a group of powerful women who can enter other realms. With her newly made friends Felicity, Ann, and Pippa, Gemma discovers a hidden world beyond their own where they can fulfill their deepest desires. However, the magic is dangerous. Gemma learns her mother was once part of the Order, and that a powerful enemy named Circe is trying to reclaim the magic. As the girls continue visiting the realms, the danger grows. When a deadly creature attacks, Pippa becomes trapped between worlds and ultimately chooses to remain in the magical realm rather than return to a life she fears. In the end, Pippa dies in the real world, and Gemma must face the consequences of unleashing powers she does not fully understand.
